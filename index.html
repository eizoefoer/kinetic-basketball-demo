Why Your Shot Feels Hard (And How to Fix It)

Most coaches teach you to muscle the ball. Clarence teaches you to move the ball — using kinetic chains, not brute force.

1. Kinetic Chain Sequencing

Learn the exact ground-up sequence: feet → hips → core → shoulder → elbow → wrist. No more "chicken wing" elbow or flat arc.

Everything You Need to Know

Is this just for elite players? I'm a rec league guy.

Absolutely not. The biomechanics are the same whether you're shooting in the NBA or at the YMCA. Clarence designed the progressions so anyone can start at their level. Phase 1 builds the foundation from scratch — no athletic background required.

What if I have shoulder/elbow issues?

This is exactly who the method was built for. The "Zero-Tension Release" eliminates the compressive forces that cause shooting pain. Many users (like R. Thompson above) returned to pain-free shooting after years of injury. Always consult your physio, but the mechanics are designed to protect joints.

How fast will I see results?

Most players feel the "click" in Phase 2 (weeks 2-3) when the kinetic chain connects. Measurable 3PT% improvement typically shows in weeks 4-6. The 8-week timeline is conservative — some see 15-20% jumps in 4 weeks. Consistency with the 10-min daily routine is the variable.

Do I need special equipment or a gym?

Just a ball and a hoop. The Phase 1-2 drills can be done without a basket (form work, shadow shooting). Phase 3+ needs a rim. No bands, weights, or machines required — your body IS the equipment when the sequence is right.

What's the difference between the PDF and videos?

The PDF is the complete reference manual — every concept, drill, progression, and troubleshooting guide in writing with diagrams. The videos show Clarence demonstrating each drill at game speed and in slow-mo from multiple angles. You get both. Use the PDF for study, videos for on-court reference.
